# ASC Challenge - 1st Place + CATIA Magic Prize

The ASC Challenge is an intercollegiate competition organised by the **Chaire Architecture des Systèmes Complexes** of Institut Polytechnique de Paris, in partnership with Dassault Aviation, Dassault Systèmes, Naval Group, KNDS, and the French Defence Innovation Agency (AID). Teams model a defence system-of-systems using MBSE methodology under CATIA Magic UAF, then defend their architectural choices in front of an industry jury.

Our team **Télécommando** - **André Poix, Florent Wallet, Mathias Anseaume, and Antonin Granados** - from Télécom Paris finished **1st** and received the **special CATIA Magic prize** at the awards ceremony held on April 28, 2026 at KNDS France in Versailles Satory.

## Challenge

Teams received an existing CATIA Magic model of **ASPIS** (Advanced Security and Protection Integrated System), a multi-domain defence system-of-systems. The goal was to evolve this model using the UAF (Unified Architecture Framework) standard to make it more adaptable to operational context changes while improving overall system efficiency.
